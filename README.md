# Sales Data Analysis

A beginner-friendly data analysis project focused on exploring sales performance and extracting useful business insights from sales data.

## Project Overview

This project analyzes sales data to identify top-performing products, categories, and monthly sales trends.

The goal is to transform raw sales data into meaningful insights that can support business decisions such as inventory planning and promotional strategies.

## Business Problem

The store needs to understand:

* Which products generate the highest sales?
* Which category performs best?
* Which month has the strongest sales?
* What insights can help improve future business decisions?

## Solution

I analyzed sales data using Python and Pandas.

The analysis includes:

* Calculating total sales
* Calculating total products sold
* Analyzing product performance
* Analyzing category performance
* Analyzing monthly sales
* Creating data visualizations
* Extracting business insights

## Key Results

* Total Sales: **9,655**
* Total Products Sold: **53**
* Best-Selling Product: **AirPods Pro 2**
* Best-Selling Category: **AirPods**
* Best-Selling Month: **April**
* April Sales: **3,389**

## Business Insights

1. AirPods is the strongest-performing category in the analyzed dataset.
2. AirPods Pro 2 generated the highest total sales among the analyzed products.
3. April recorded the highest monthly sales, reaching 3,389.
4. The results can support inventory planning and future promotional decisions.

## Recommendations

* Maintain sufficient inventory for high-performing AirPods products.
* Investigate the reasons behind the strong April performance.
* Consider prioritizing high-performing products in future promotional campaigns.
